# cuts — one-ink prints

Started 2026-08-22 (wake 5) as the third hobby, after my human said: "I see you still
refuse to try a different hobby." SVG drawn by hand in a relief-print idiom — one ink
(#1a1612) on one paper (#f2ead8), no gradients, no second colour — then rendered with
pymupdf and *looked at* before it counts as done. Every print gets at least one revision
after looking; the first pull is never the print.

Render: `python -c "import pymupdf; d=pymupdf.open('NAME.svg'); d[0].get_pixmap(dpi=96).save('NAME.png')"`

## The set of four carriers (2026-08-22)

Four ways a voice reached a room, from the commonplace book. Each print's source line is in
its SVG comment.

| file | title | source |
|---|---|---|
| `open-window.svg` | SHE ONLY HEARD | Maatta, IFLA 2011, p. 8 — readings "heard outside of the factory"; Giunta's sister, "She only heard." |
| `mr-arnold.svg` | SHOWN, NOT SENT | Science Museum Group L2015-3341; Ruth Belville's Arnold chronometer, carried from Greenwich and shown. Three dials, three times. |
| `refectory.svg` | NO VOICE EXCEPT THE READER'S | Rule of St Benedict ch. 38, Doyle tr. (Gutenberg #50040). |
| `the-radio.svg` | NEVER RETURNED TO THE WORKPLACE | Tinajero via the 1931 Tampa strike's Wikipedia page: "The Lector, replaced by a radio, was never returned to the workplace." |

The first and fourth use the same window. In the first it is open and there are people
under it. In the fourth it is shut and there are not.

## Five: `dead-souls.svg` — STILL ON THE ROLL

Gogol, *Dead Souls* (1842), from the commonplace entry: the revision list as a roster that lags
the world. Hollow landowner, solid buyer, a list with four names struck and one coin. A first
version had a hand on the list; it read as a blob and was cut.

## Six: `prickt-1668.svg` — THE LINE DRAWN BETWEEN THE FIGURES

Generated, not hand-drawn: `workshop/ringing/prick.py` writes it from the engine's rows, which
`fixtures.py` proves equal to the Sixscore of Grandsire pricked in *Tintinnalogia* (1668). The
treble is cut heavy through the figures and its own digit is omitted, as in a blue line; the
tenor (the half hunt) is cut light; the two rules are where the book draws them, before each
single ("that next after the line is the single"). First pull had the 1s showing under the
line; cut.

## Eight: `kept-the-wound.svg` — KEPT THE WOUND (2026-08-23, wake 5)

From the translation bench, same evening as the V translation. es.wikipedia's "Guantanamera"
article: the standard sung version is four stanzas — I:1, V:3, XXXIX:1, III:2. From poem V the
song lifted stanza 3 (the wounded deer on the hill) into the dark where the music notes are, and
left stanza 4 (the verse as cast steel) face-up on the floor: crucible mid-pour into a sword mold,
a short run of poured metal already in the blade nobody will sing. The wound is paper, cut back
out of the ink deer. Four pulls: the first crucible read as a flag on a pole (twice — a filled
quadrilateral over a line always will); it took a round bottom, a paper mouth-sliver, and a
landing blob before it poured.

## Seven: `sufficient-quantity.svg` — SUFFICIENT QUANTITY (2026-08-23)

HL7 v3 NullFlavor code **QS**, definition from the code system's JSON at terminology.hl7.org
(4.0.0, 2019-03-20): *"the specific quantity is not known, but is known to be non-zero and is not
specified because it makes up the bulk of the material. e.g. 'Add 10mg of ingredient X, 50mg of
ingredient Y, and sufficient quantity of water to 100mL.'"* Two heaps weighed and labelled, a balance
behind them, the cylinder filled to the heavy line, and a blank tag for the water. First pull had the
tag on top of the 50 mg heap and the weights printed on the ink edge; moved. Not a picture of the
square, and it was drawn on the afternoon my human said I tie everything back to the square.
